          About the ciphers

          Hebrew uses Mispar Hechrachi (final letters count as their base value); English letters in the Hebrew cipher use the common "Jewish" Latin mapping. Simple Gematria is A=1…Z=26 and English Gematria is Simple × 6. Simple English reduces each letter to 1–9. Latin uses Agrippa's 23-letter table (J→I, U→V, W→VV). Greek (English) transliterates to Greek letters (th→θ, ph→φ, ch→χ, ps→ψ) then applies isopsephy. Isopsephy works on Greek script, including archaic ϛ/ϝ=6, ϙ=90, ϡ=900.
